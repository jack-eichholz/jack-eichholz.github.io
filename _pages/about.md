Hello! I am a PhD Student in Political Science at The George Washington University specializing in American Politics and Research Methods. My interests include political parties, congressional primary elections, and elite endorsements. Specifically, my work focuses on how party elites influence congressional primary trajectories and outcomes through endorsements.

My projects include a comprehensive database of all elite endorsements made in the 2026 congressional primary elections hosted on [VoteHub.com](https://votehub.com/2026-endorsements). The project uses a novel weighting system to account for differences in endorsers’ public visibility. I have also written for VoteHub, translating my research into accessible general audience analysis of the role endorsements play in congressional races.

I can be reached at [jack.eichholz@gwu.edu](mailto:jack.eichholz@gwu.edu), or feel free to follow me on Twitter [@JackEichholz](https://twitter.com/JackEichholz).
