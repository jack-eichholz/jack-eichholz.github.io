Featured project · VoteHub

2026 Congressional Endorsement Tracker

Who is backing whom—and what does that support tell us about congressional primaries?

I maintain a publicly accessible database of political elite endorsements in the 2026 U.S. House primary elections. Published on VoteHub, the tracker brings together candidate-level endorsement data and a novel measure of endorser visibility to help readers explore how elite support is distributed across races nationwide.

Explore the endorsement tracker →

My role

I collect, organize, and maintain the endorsement data, keeping candidate and endorser records updated as campaigns develop. I also use the data to examine patterns in elite support and communicate those findings to a general audience through VoteHub analysis.

The project combines my research interests in political parties, congressional primaries, and elite influence with the practical work of maintaining an accessible public data resource. Visualization by Luke Wines.

What makes the tracker different?

Endorsements weighted by visibility. Endorsers differ in their public prominence. The tracker combines recent Wikipedia pageviews with bonuses for current or former office to distinguish support from political figures with different levels of visibility.

Candidate and endorser perspectives. Readers can search candidates to explore their endorsement coalitions or search endorsers to see where they have directed their support.

Geographic relevance. The scoring system gives an additional boost to endorsements when the endorser and candidate are from the same state.

Why it matters

Congressional primaries often present voters with several candidates from the same party. Endorsements offer a window into how political elites navigate those contests: which candidates they support, how coalitions develop, and whether elite support corresponds with electoral success. The tracker makes these relationships visible and provides a foundation for my ongoing research.
